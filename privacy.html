Effective: 2 October 2026 · Previous version: 29 July 2026

In short. Agon stores your body weight, meals and workouts so you can look back at them. You can export all of your data at any time, and permanently delete your account from within the app.

Data that stays on your phone

Some data never reaches our servers and is stored only on your device: your settings (language, theme, accent colour, font, notification preferences), the cached daily verse, and the daily figures shown by the home screen widget and the in-workout Live Activity. Deleting the app removes them.

What we do NOT collect

  • No analytics, no advertising identifier, no behavioural tracking.
  • We do not request access to your contacts, location or microphone. The camera and photo library are used only when you open them for the AI estimate.
  • We do not send push notifications from a server: reminders are scheduled by your phone itself (see section 7).
  • No cookies — this is a mobile app, not a website.
  • We do not collect data from other apps beyond Apple Health / Health Connect.

About meal photos

  • Analysis: our own server (a Supabase Edge Function) forwards the photo to the Anthropic API. The result is a list of items and a suggested meal name, which you can edit before saving.
  • Storage: when you save the estimate, the photo is stored in a private bucket in the EU, in a folder belonging to your account. It has no public link: the app shows it through a signed link that expires after one hour, and the storage access rules do not let other users reach it.
  • Deletion: the photo is deleted when you delete its group, when you delete the last item of the group, or when you delete your account. If you do not want any photo kept, use the AI estimate with a text description instead — then no photo is taken or saved.

About Anthropic

Anthropic processes the images and text you submit as our processor. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, content submitted through the API is not used to train their models, but it may be retained for a limited period for abuse prevention. The current terms are set out in Anthropic's privacy policy.

Because Anthropic operates in the United States, this is a transfer outside the EU. It relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), which form part of Anthropic's commercial terms. If you do not want this, do not use the AI features — every other part of the app works fully without them.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your data — including photos — is kept for as long as your account exists, or until you delete the entry.
  • When you delete your account, all of your data and photos are deleted immediately from the live database and file storage.
  • Deleted data leaves the hosting provider's backups within 30 days at the latest.
  • We keep an anonymous log of the deletion (date + "requested by user") that contains neither an identifier nor an email address. It is kept for 3 years for legal accountability.

6. Security

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • The database uses row-level security (RLS): queries cannot reach other users' rows, even at the database level. The same applies to photo storage: everyone can reach only their own folder.
  • Photos are not public; they are displayed through short-lived signed links.
  • The AI provider's key lives only on the server and is not in the app binary.

7. Notifications, widget, Live Activity

  • Notifications: reminders (e.g. logging, workouts, weekly recap) are scheduled locally by your phone based on your settings. No data is sent to a server for this. The system asks for permission, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time.
  • Widget and Live Activity: if you add them, your daily calorie and workout figures appear on the home or lock screen. This is stored locally on the phone; however, anyone who looks at your screen can see it — if that bothers you, do not use the widget.

8. Children

Agon is not for users under 16. You confirm your age when you sign up, and the app also checks the date of birth you enter in the onboarding. If we learn that we hold data of someone under 16, we delete the account and the data.

9. Not a medical service

Agon is an informational and self-tracking tool, not a medical device. The values shown — including calorie targets, AI estimates, the AI workout plan, estimated calories burned and BMI — are estimates, not diagnoses. AI estimates can be wrong, which is why you can always review and correct them before saving. Consult a doctor or dietitian before making health decisions.

10. Safety limits

The app includes eating disorder safeguards. These calculations run only on your phone — no extra data is sent to the server and your behaviour is not analysed.

  • With a BMI below 18.5, a weight loss goal cannot be selected.
  • Below a BMI of 17.5, only logging is offered, without goal planning.
  • The daily calorie target cannot be set below 1,200 kcal (women) / 1,500 kcal (men).
  • A target weight below the healthy range cannot be saved.

These limits are based on WHO and Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics guidance. Under Settings → Safety and wellbeing you will find official support services.

11. Daily verse

The daily Bible verse is downloaded from our server from a shared verse collection. No data about you is used for this, and we do not record which verse you read. The day's verse is cached on your phone so it also shows offline.
